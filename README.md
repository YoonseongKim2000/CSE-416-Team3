## Problem Statement
**Problem**
- Nowadays, AI generate images can be seen in great quantity on the internet. Most of the time, the images
are used harmlessly but bad actors will us this technology to fulfuill their agenda. This can be anything
from deepfakes or AI generated images to tell fake news or selling AI work as human work. In the beginning, it was possible to tell what images has been generated by AI but now it has become increasingly
difficult to do so.

**Solution**
- Our solution is to develop a web application that uses AI services to assist the process of analyzing images to determine if they have been generated by AI or made by a person. Our platform will also provide users with the ability to see how the AI is determining an image is AI using ViT's attention. In addition, if time permits, add a function where the we can hash images and track them so users can see how rampant an image is on the internet.
